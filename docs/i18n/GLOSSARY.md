## Decisions (final, confirmed by the user)
1. "sir" → «сэр».
2. Aymu's accent: «German» devoicing (STYLE §5).
3. Weapons officer = «канонир»; Scanning department = «Сканеры».
4. "Thermos" weapon → «термос» (masculine; keeps the original's thermos pun), in all forms and labels.
5. Officer genders not stated by the game: Yeela, Bru, Churglik male; Nargo female (accepted).
6. Splyteux → «Сплайтё» (accepted).
